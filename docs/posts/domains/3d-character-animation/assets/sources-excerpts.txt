[glTF 2.0 Specification, Skins / Animations]
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/blob/9d23a5a5b1d10f22d1554e847f2a1a3f24222392/specification/2.0/Specification.adoc
glTF 2.0 mesh primitives support Linear Blend Skinning via skin objects, joint hierarchies, and designated vertex attributes.
glTF supports articulated and skinned animation via key frame animations of nodes' transforms.

[Spine Runtimes 4.2, IkConstraint.ts]
https://github.com/EsotericSoftware/spine-runtimes/blob/5cb78e77d3711ddcd80c7bd043bb11cf70fcc88c/spine-ts/spine-core/src/IkConstraint.ts
An IK constraint adjusts the rotation of 1 or 2 constrained bones so the tip of the last bone is as close to the target bone as possible.
Controls the bend direction of the IK bones, either 1 or -1.

[threejs-procedural-animals, README / AUTHORING]
https://github.com/majidmanzarpour/threejs-procedural-animals/blob/c95ae49346aa8e140a924376cec6cf0073d99512/README.md
24 animals for three.js, generated entirely in code: no models, no textures, no animation clips.
sculpted from signed distance fields, meshed, rigged, skinned (dual-quaternion), covered in fur, feathers or scales, and animated with IK gaits and actions.
five quality tiers, from `hero` (~120k vertices) to `crowd` (~6k vertices, one draw call).
https://github.com/majidmanzarpour/threejs-procedural-animals/blob/c95ae49346aa8e140a924376cec6cf0073d99512/docs/AUTHORING.md
`k` is the smooth-union blend radius, and 0 gives a hard union.
